3.4 Electrical SafetyPDF p.18

After performing AC and DC double-side disconnect operations on the energy storage system, wait at least 15 minutes before opening the front door to perform maintenance, overhaul, or other functions on the energy storage system.

When the energy storage system is electrically connected for a test run or other operations, use high-quality measuring equipment with a range and accuracy that meets the site requirements to ensure that the electrical parameters meet the requirements. Ensure that measuring equipment is correctly connected and used according to standards to prevent arcs and other hazards.

Even if all external circuits are disconnected, high voltage may still exist between the positive and negative terminals of the energy storage battery pack. If measurements must be conducted under charged conditions, use dedicated instruments, and ensure that only qualified professionals perform the tests.

If the energy storage system operates abnormally or or requires maintenance or an, shut down the equipment and confirm that the energy storage system completely stops running before proceeding with any maintenance or repair work.

Après avoir effectué des opérations de déconnexion double face AC et DC sur le système de stockage d’énergie, veuillez attendre au moins 15 minutes avant d’ouvrir la porte avant pour effectuer la maintenance, la révision ou d’autres fonctions sur le système de stockage d’énergie.

Lorsque le système de stockage d’énergie est connecté électriquement lors d’un essai ou d’autres opérations, il est nécessaire d’utiliser des équipements de mesure de haute qualité avec une portée et une précision qui répondent aux exigences du site pour s ’ assurer que les paramètres électriques répondent aux exigences. Assurez-vous que l’équipement de mesure est correctement connecté et utilisé conformément aux normes pour éviter les arcs électriques et autres dangers.

Même si tous les circuits externes sont déconnectés, une haute tension existe toujours entre les bornes positive et négative de la batterie de stockage d’énergie. 3.6 Electrostatic ProtectionPDF p.20

Accumulation of static electricity can result in electric shocks, fire, explosion, failure and damage of electronic devices, and other safety hazards. The energy storage system contains circuit boards and other static-sensitive components. To mitigate the risks associated with static electricity, operators must implement static protection measures, minimize static electricity generation, accelerate the dissipation of static electricity, and neutralize static electricity.

The prevention methods include but are not limited to:

  • During replacement, all equipment devices that have not yet been installed should be retained in bags with anti-static shielding, and temporarily removed equipment devices should be placed on foam pads with anti-static features.
  • Do not touch soldering points, pins, or exposed circuits.

EMC related cautions The system’s emission characteristics make it suitable for use in industrial areas, meaning Class A electromagnetic environment. If used in a residential environment , where Class B is typically required, this equipment may not provide adequate protection to radio- frequency communication services. User may need to implement mitigation measures, such as relocating or re-orienting the equipment.

Mains power quality should conform to the standards of a specialized industrial distribution network. For users requiring uninterrupted operation during or after power mains interruptions, it is recommended to connect the system's information storage components to an uninterruptible power supply (UPS) or a backup power source.

Some portable RF communications equipment such as high-power walkie-talkie may ddegrade the electromagnetic performance of the system. If the essential system performance is lost or impaired due to Electromagnetic disturbances, maintain a safe distance between high-power transmitters and the system, and contact customer service to address the issue.

4.3 Transportation RequirementsPDF p.24

The equipment in the ESS has been installed and securely fixed inside the container before shipment, allowing the container to be lifted and transported as a single unit.

  • During loading, unloading and transportation, ensure compliance with the safety regulations governing ESS container operations in the country where the project is located.
  • The ESS container and any machinery used in the operation should be properly maintained.
  • All personnel involved in loading, unloading and bolting must be trained accordingly, especially safety training.
  • The mechanical parameters of the battery prefabrication pods, including external dimensions and weight, must be accounted for throughout the loading, unloading and transportation process.
  • The following conditions must be fulfilled for the transportation of ESS container:

– The doors of all cabinets in the battery container are securely locked.

– Select a suitable crane or lifting tool according to the site conditions. The selected device must have sufficient load capacity, boom length and swivel radius.

– When lifting the ESS container, it is recommended to use a single crane.

– Additional towing devices may be required if moving for slopes.

– Clear all obstacles that may exist during the movement, such as trees, cables, etc.

– The ESS container should be transported and moved under favorable weather conditions whenever possible.

– Use warning signs or tape to restrict access to the lifting and transportation area and prevent accidents.

When transporting by land, use ropes to secure the corner fittings on top of the ESS container to the vehicle. Ensure the ESS container remains stable and the inclination angle is minimized during transportation.

4.4 Storage RequirementsPDF p.25

  • To prevent possible condensation or its bottom soaking from rainwater in the rainy season. The ESS should be stored on higher ground.
  • Raise the container base are necessary to suit site conditions. The specific height should be reasonably determined based on site geological and meteorological conditions of the site.
  • Store the ESS on dry, flat, and stable ground with sufficient load-bearing capacity and free of vegetation.
  • Ensure the container doors and all internal equipment are securely locked.
  • Storage environment temperature: -35~60℃.
  • Long-term storage is not recommended because it may cause a decrease in battery capacity. Even when stored at the recommended storage temperature, irreversible capacity fading will still occur over time. The longer the storage period, the greater the capacity fade. Refer to the technical protocol for specific capacity fade rates.
  • The relative humidity should be between 0~95%, without condensation.
  • Protect the air inlet and outlet of the ESS to prevent rain water, sand, and dust from entering the container.
  • Perform periodic inspections.
  • Check the container and the inner equipment for damage at least once per month.
  • For containers stored for more than six months, inspect the interior by opening the door to ensure there is no visible condensation. Verify that the container and internal equipment are undamaged.
  • Inspect the system after powering it on and initiating startup procedures.
  • If required, request professional assistance for testing before installation.
  • For BESS systems stored for more than six months under the above conditions, a charge and discharge cycle must be performed to bring the system SOC to 30%-40%. The storage period begins from the date of departure from the HyperStrong factory.

7.10 UPS CautionsPDF p.75

CAUTION

Beware of electric shock, explosion or arcing

  • Electrical equipment installation, operation, repair, and maintenance of electrical equipment must be carried out by qualified personnel.
  • Wear appropriate personal protective equipment and follow electrical safety operation standards.
  • Turn off all power supplies of the UPS system when operating or maintaining equipment.
  • Before operating the UPS system, check for dangerous voltages across all terminals, including protective grounding.
  • The UPS contains an internal power supply. Hazardous voltages may be present even when disconnected from utility power. Before installing or servicing the UPS system, ensure the unit is OFF and disconnected from the utility power and battery connections. Wait five minutes for the capacitors to discharge before turning on the UPS.
  • The UPS system must be isolated from its upstream power source by a disconnecting device (disconnect, switch) that complies with local codes. The isolation device must be in a conspicuous and easily accessible location.
  • The UPS must be properly grounded. Due to the possibility of a considerable leakage current, ground wires must be connected first.

Failure to follow the instructions may result in severe consequences such as personal injury or death.

CAUTION

Beware of equipment damage

  • Do not install the batteries until the system is ready to be energized. The recommended time from battery installation to UPS power-up should not exceed 72 hours.
  • Adhere to the charging requirements, the storage time of the battery must not exceed 6 months. If the UPS system remains powered off for an extended period, It is recommended to charge the battery of UPS system at least once per month for 24 hours per charge. This periodic charging helps prevent irreversible damage.

Failure to follow the instructions may result in irreversible damage to the UPS.

9.1 Emergency HandlingPDF p.92

If any of the following conditions (including but not limited to) occur, immediately stop using the ESS container and the adjacent battery container to prevent electric shock, fire, or explosion.

In Case of Fire:

In case of electrical equipment catches fire, immediately cut off the power supply and use dry powder or carbon dioxide fire extinguishers to extinguish the fire.

If there is an open flame outside the ESS container, immediately move away from the ESS system and contact the local fire emergency services for assistance.

After emergency troubleshooting, stand and observe for a specific period. If no abnormality occurs, proceed to the next step of troubleshooting.

Typical ESS emergencies and anomalies:

  • Abnormal sound, spark, odor or other irregularities occurs during the operation or maintenance.
  • Smoke or fire in the container.
  • Expansion, rupture, melting, deformation of the battery module.
  • External or internal short circuit.
  • Extreme weather conditions, such as rainstorms and flash floods.
  • Thermal runaway occurs during use, indicated by a battery temperature exceeding 85°C.
  • Structural damage that has not yet caused a severe accident.
  • The coolant of the chiller leaks.
  • The auxiliary power supply is abnormal or the communication is interrupted .
  • Damage to the main structure of the battery container or IP protection of the battery system.
  • Loose or burnt marks on electrical connections.
  • Insulation fault alarm. If triggered, stop using immediately and verify whether the alarm is false.

If any the above situation occurs (not limited to the above case), resulting in the ESS's dysfunction, contact our professional maintenance team or technical support immediately.

If the situation is critical, activate the contingency plan (refer to the maintenance manual), and notify first responders or fire services.
